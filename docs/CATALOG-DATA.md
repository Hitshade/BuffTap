# BuffTap catalog data

Catalog.lua is generated by `tools/build_catalog.py` from Blizzard's WoW Forever
1.60.1.70009 client DB2 exports. No addon catalog is a generator input.
The compatibility spec preserves BuffTap's public saved keys, order, enabled
defaults and recipient choices. Names, ranked spell membership, levels and group
reagents come from the client tables. Damage-trigger spells are excluded;
learned-spell verification is still required at runtime.

Export SpellName (save as spellnames.csv), Spell, SpellLevels, SkillLineAbility,
SpellEffect and SpellReagents using:
`https://wago.tools/db2/<Table>/csv?build=1.60.1.70009`.
Run `python tools/build_catalog.py --data <export-folder>` from the repository.
Input hashes and the generated family manifest are in tools/catalog-evidence.json.
The raw exports are development inputs, not shipped with the addon.

The rebuild corrects omitted Seal of Righteousness rank 20288; all seven available
Seal of Fury ranks and five Trueshot ranks; Demon Armor's rank-1 spell 706 and
subsequent client levels; and poison creation/application recognition. Triggered
damage spell 11354 is no longer a learned poison-application candidate.
Catalog loading constructs coverage references once; no CSV parsing, network
request or database scan happens in the client.

This documents an independent data-driven replacement, not a claim of a clean-room
process: the prior catalog was available during compatibility review. Preserving
setting identifiers does not make the earlier catalog the source of client facts.
Historical releases, their notices and local provenance records are retained.
