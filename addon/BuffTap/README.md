# BuffTap

**Smart buff reminders and effortless mouse-wheel buffing for all classes in WoW Forever.**

BuffTap detects missing or expiring buffs, follows your priorities, and prepares the next spell or item. **Scroll to buff, then keep adventuring.** Prefer a different control? Use a custom keybind, mouse button, or the clickable reminder.

BuffTap automates detection and preparation; every cast or item use still requires your input. Designed for out-of-combat maintenance, with event-driven updates and cached information.

## 🛡️ Buff yourself, your party, or your raid

Choose what to maintain, who receives it, and when to refresh it.

- **Personal and friendly-target buffing** with independent settings and class-aware Paladin target choices.
- **Per-buff raid-group and class filters**, priorities, and refresh thresholds.
- **Smart group spells** that check who still needs the buff before spending reagents.
- **Paladin blessing assignments and ordered priorities** with separate party/raid plans, uniform or per-class choices, subgroup overrides, visual assignment icons, and player exceptions.

## 👥 Nearby buffing & quick party invites

Share buffs with nearby players from a **compact, movable window**, with class-coloured names and missing-buff icons. **Players needing an eligible buff appear first**. Click a player or use your normal mouse-wheel or key binding to apply the next buff.

- **Stable, fixed-size list** with five visible rows, a scrollbar and support for up to 40 detected players.
- **Optional all-players view** keeps nearby strangers listed after buffing, with a small header icon to switch views.
- **Quick party invites** beside player names, or suggestions to your group leader when the game offers that action. Invite shortcuts work in either view.
- **Independent controls** for nearby buffing, the player list and invitation buttons in the **Nearby tab**. **Shift-left-click the minimap icon** to toggle the window.
- **Your existing preferences apply**: Target settings for strangers and Groups settings for current party or raid members. Group members leave the list when no eligible buff is queued.
- **Quiet buffing**, with no extra reminder icon or notification ding.

**Friendly player nameplates must be enabled**, normally with **Shift+V**. Nearby starts disabled. Every cast and invitation requires your input, and the window hides during combat. PvP and recipient-combat checks continue to protect buff casting.

## ⚔️ Keep your weapons ready

Maintain **Rogue poisons, Shaman imbues, supported Mage imbue scrolls, Warlock Firestones and Spellstones, oils, sharpening stones, and weightstones** through the same convenient binding.

Choose poisons and compatible oils/stones independently for each hand; Shaman imbues, Mage scrolls and Warlock stones use the main hand. Set your refresh timing and decide whether different existing effects may be replaced. Preferences remain saved when supplies run out.

Weapon application is optional and starts disabled.

## 🧪 Consumables without the bag search

Select supported **food buffs, flasks, elixirs and selected utility consumables** using a searchable picker with an in-bags filter. Keep a preferred item selected or use supported automatic choices.

Optional **low-stock warnings** cover selected buff and weapon consumables, with configurable thresholds, private alerts, sounds, and ready-check summaries. Consumable management and stock warnings start disabled.

## 🔥 Camping & sleeping bag

Keep Camp Benefits and your sleeping bag's Well-Rested bonus in view with an optional, separately movable reminder. Check stacks and timers, snooze reminders, and open a compact tray for carried camp items and profession recipes. Customize its icon and text independently. Every craft or placement requires your click; food stays under Consumables.

## ✨ Helpful extras, when you want them

Enable **Hunter/Warlock pet readiness, personal Healthstone creation, Soulstone upkeep for you or your group's healer, gathering-tracker reminders, or party buff-coverage information**. Dismiss unwanted reminders, reduce repeated stronger-effect errors, or optionally thank players who buff you while questing solo.

Helpers are opt-in, so you choose how much assistance you want. Paladins can also maintain an explicitly assigned Oath of Light recipient when talented.

## 🎨 Make it your own

Open **Customization** to adjust your binding, appearance, sounds and quick access. Use arrows to arrange buff priorities. Move and resize the reminder, including alongside Blizzard Edit Mode, and adjust opacity, label fonts/colors, timers, glow style, color and speed. Both reminders have independent text font, size, color, outline and spacing controls. Choose separate reminder and low-stock sounds from game assets or installed SharedMedia packs, search and preview them, and control alert frequency. Reminders pause while mounted; optionally pause them in cities and inns too.

Use first-use guidance to get started, and right-click the minimap icon for quick controls or a temporary pause. Open settings from the **draggable minimap button** or enable an optional **LibDataBroker display** alongside, or in place of, the standard reminder. Native game icons and automatic Spanish, German, French, and Brazilian Portuguese localization keep the interface familiar; untranslated text falls back to English.

## Solo, Party and Raid preferences

Use the selectors inside Buffs, Weapons and Consumables to prepare different choices for each group context. Party and Raid follow Solo until a preference is changed. Use Solo settings restores inheritance for that section. Group context switches automatically out of combat; choosing a selector only changes which preferences you are editing. Groups has separate five-player Party and Raid preferences for recipient filters, enablement and group-spell thresholds. Paladins retain their separate blessing plans and Greater Blessing controls. Existing group preferences are preserved in both; neither Groups setup inherits Solo.

## Get started

Install **BuffTap** in your client's `Interface/AddOns` folder, open **`/bt`**, choose your buffs, and start scrolling. Nearby has its own settings page; friendly player nameplates are required.
