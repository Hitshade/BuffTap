**Smart buff reminders and effortless mouse-wheel buffing for all classes in WoW Forever.**

BuffTap detects missing or expiring buffs, follows your priorities, and prepares the next spell or item. **Scroll to buff, then keep adventuring.** Prefer a different control? Use a custom keybind, mouse button, or the clickable reminder.

BuffTap automates detection and preparation; every cast or item use still requires your input. Designed for out-of-combat maintenance, with event-driven updates and cached information.

## Nearby player buffing (optional)

Buff nearby players without manually hunting through targets. Enable the optional **Nearby window** to see eligible players who need your buffs, with **class-coloured names and missing-buff icons**. Click a name or use your normal mouse-wheel or key binding to apply the next buff.

- **Compact, movable and lockable**, with stable rows and scrolling for longer lists.
- **Uses your Target buff preferences**, including class-aware Paladin blessing choices.
- Enable **Nearby buffing in the Target tab**, or **Shift-left-click the minimap icon** to toggle it.
- **Quiet nearby reminders**, with no extra reminder icon or notification ding.
- **Out-of-combat use**, with checks for recipient combat state and your PvP protection setting.

**Friendly player nameplates must be enabled**, normally with **Shift+V**. Nearby buffing starts disabled, and every cast still requires your click or binding.

## 🛡️ Buff yourself, your party, or your raid

Choose what to maintain, who receives it, and when to refresh it.

- **Personal and friendly-target buffing** with independent settings and class-aware Paladin target choices.
- **Per-buff raid-group and class filters**, priorities, and refresh thresholds.
- **Smart group spells** that check who still needs the buff before spending reagents.
- **Paladin blessing assignments and ordered priorities** with separate party/raid plans, uniform or per-class choices, subgroup overrides, visual assignment icons, player exceptions and independent Salvation protection.

## ⚔️ Keep your weapons ready

Maintain **Rogue poisons, Shaman imbues, supported Mage imbue scrolls, Warlock Firestones and Spellstones, oils, sharpening stones, and weightstones** through the same convenient binding.

Choose poisons and compatible oils/stones independently for each hand; Shaman imbues, Mage scrolls and Warlock stones use the main hand. Set your refresh timing and decide whether different existing effects may be replaced. Preferences remain saved when supplies run out.

Weapon application is optional and starts disabled.

## 🧪 Consumables without the bag search

Select supported **food buffs, flasks, and elixirs** using a searchable picker with an in-bags filter. Keep a preferred item selected or use supported automatic choices.

Optional **low-stock warnings** cover selected buff and weapon consumables, with configurable thresholds, private alerts, sounds, and ready-check summaries. Consumable management and stock warnings start disabled.

## ✨ Helpful extras, when you want them

Enable **Hunter/Warlock pet readiness, personal Healthstone creation, Soulstone upkeep for you or your group's healer, gathering-tracker reminders, or party buff-coverage information**. Dismiss unwanted reminders, reduce repeated stronger-effect errors, or optionally thank players who buff you while questing solo.

Helpers are opt-in, so you choose how much assistance you want.

## 🎨 Make it your own

Open **Customization** to adjust your binding, appearance, sounds and quick access. Use arrows to arrange buff priorities. Move and resize the reminder, including alongside Blizzard Edit Mode, and adjust opacity, label fonts/colors, timers, glow, and pulse. Choose separate reminder and low-stock sounds from game assets or installed SharedMedia packs, search and preview them, and control alert frequency. Reminders pause while mounted; optionally pause them in cities and inns too.

Use first-use guidance to get started, and right-click the minimap icon for quick controls or a temporary pause. Open settings from the **draggable minimap button** or enable an optional **LibDataBroker display** alongside, or in place of, the standard reminder. Native game icons and automatic Spanish, German, French, and Brazilian Portuguese localization keep the interface familiar; untranslated text falls back to English.

## Paladin group setup

In Groups, choose Party or Raid. By class uses class assignments and optional player exceptions. Same blessing for everyone applies one blessing to the entire party or selected raid group and temporarily ignores those saved exceptions. Raid groups inherit the raid defaults unless you create an override. Excluded raid groups are skipped, and Greater Blessings only apply when all affected same-class recipients agree.

Party and raid plans are character-specific, including when switching shared profiles. The explicit same-class copy option includes both plans; other classes cannot import them. Copy party assignments changes raid defaults, retaining existing subgroup overrides. Salvation has separate permission and recipient controls.

## Named profiles

Use **Profiles** to share bindings, appearance, sounds and general preferences across characters. Buff and class settings remain on each character when switching profiles. **Copy from profile** optionally imports a saved setup from a same-class character; incompatible classes are not offered. Characters using one profile share subsequent general preference changes. Create a separate profile with **Copy current preferences** to keep independent settings.

## Get started

Install **BuffTap** in your client's `Interface/AddOns` folder, open **`/bt`**, choose your buffs, and start scrolling.
